What is a Payment Reference?

Your payment reference is the identifier generated by your bank after the transfer.

It may appear on your bank receipt or banking app as:

  • Transaction Reference
  • Transaction ID
  • Session ID
  • Transfer Reference
  • Payment Reference

Copy the reference exactly as it appears on your bank transaction record.

Do not enter: your ATM PIN, OTP, card number, banking password, or account password.

Enter the transaction/reference number shown on your bank receipt or banking app.

STEP 3

Payment Verification

Course access is granted only after Machpadaco verifies your payment.

01 · Payment Review Your payment reference and payment evidence are checked.
02 · Confirmation Your enrolment is approved after successful payment verification.
03 · Account Access The verified course is added to your existing Machpadaco student account.
04 · Protected Learning You can log in with your existing account and access the verified course.
STEP 4

Access Your Course

Once your payment has been verified, your existing Machpadaco student account will receive access to the course you enrolled in.
