WHO WE ARE

Building Practical Solutions Across Multiple Sectors

Machpadaco Global Services is a CAC-registered business (RC: 8489988, Registered May 16, 2025, Status: ACTIVE) based in Umuahia, Abia State, Nigeria. Founded by Engr. Ejike Njuwa, we provide professional website and software development, digital skills and ICT training, digital marketing, phone engineering and repair services, and property management virtual assistance. We help businesses establish and improve their online presence, while supporting property owners and managers with remote administrative and short-term rental operations. Our services help individuals and businesses build practical digital skills, improve operations and grow through technology.
